PDF2JPG Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

PDF2JPG Pro converts PDF files to JPG images directly in your browser. This page explains what data the app handles and what is stored.

Files stay on your device

The app does not upload your PDF files or generated JPG images to a server operated by this project. PDF rendering and image generation happen locally in your browser.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require accounts or login.
  • We do not collect payment information.
  • We do not store your uploaded PDF files.
  • We do not store your generated JPG images.

Browser storage

As a Progressive Web App, PDF2JPG Pro may use your browser's Cache Storage to save application files such as JavaScript, CSS, icons, the service worker, PDF.js assets, and offline pages. This helps the app load faster and launch offline after the first production load.

The app may also use localStorage to remember basic install-state hints. These values are not your PDF content.

Third-party services

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers may process standard technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and request time according to their own policies.

A Google site verification meta tag may appear in the home page so the site owner can verify ownership in Google Search Console. That tag does not, by itself, collect your PDF content.

Security limits

Browser-based processing reduces exposure because files do not need to leave your device. You should still avoid opening untrusted files on devices or browsers you do not control.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at the project repository: github.com/yapweijun1996/pdf-to-jpg.
